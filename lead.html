Leadership

Lead yourself first, then others.

Why This Track Matters

We want our children to stand on their own, to stay calm when life gets hard, make good decisions, and pick themselves up after they fall. We teach them to lead themselves first, then others. Above all, to do what's right, even when it's hard. The Gita calls this dharma, the foundation of real leadership.

What We Teach

Leading ourself and others, goal setting, decision making, staying calm under pressure, bouncing back from failure, building relationships, resolving conflict, and managing time. These are the skills that build young leaders.

The Age-by-Age Progression

Leadership is built in layers. Here's how it grows across the three-year journey.

Year 1

Foundations

Ages 10–12
  • Naming personal values and what "the right thing" means to them
  • Setting and following through on small personal goals
  • Practicing calm responses instead of reacting when frustrated
  • Learning to make simple decisions independently, with support
Year 2

Building

Ages 13–15
  • Taking ownership of a group project or team role from start to finish
  • Practicing structured decision-making under real deadlines and pressure
  • Resolving disagreements with peers using specific conflict-resolution steps
  • Building daily habits of discipline: time management, follow-through, accountability
Year 3

Mastery

Ages 16–18
  • Leading a team or initiative and owning the outcome, not just the tasks
  • Mentoring younger students through the same journey
  • Navigating high-stakes decisions like college applications, jobs, and ethical dilemmas using a clear values framework
  • Bouncing back from setbacks like rejection, failure, and criticism with resilience
