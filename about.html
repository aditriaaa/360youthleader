About the Program

The 360° Youth Leader helps young people grow into confident, grounded, value-driven leaders — not just academically successful, but ready for life.
← Back to home

Why this exists

Most youth programs focus on one narrow outcome: academic achievement, grades, or a single skill. But real life asks for more. It asks for emotional balance, clarity, self-awareness, communication, money sense, ethics, resilience, and purpose.

The 360° Youth Leader is designed to help children build those capabilities early, in a way that is practical, personal, and rooted in meaning.

What we teach

Leadership

How to lead yourself first, then others — with calm, clarity, and integrity.

Life Skills

From communication and problem-solving to personal finance and self-defense.

Inner Growth

Resilience, emotional awareness, mental steadiness, and purpose-rooted decision making.

Grounded in timeless wisdom

The program draws on the practical guidance of the Gita — especially the themes of clarity, duty, self-mastery, and conscious action — while making them feel immediately relevant to modern child and teen life.
