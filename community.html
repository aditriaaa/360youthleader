Community & Character

Sanskaar, seva and choosing the right community.

Why This Track Matters

Sanskaar matters. Seva matters. We want our children to be kind, respectful, and rooted. We want them to know who they are, where they come from, and how to lift others. And we help them choose their satsang, or the company they keep, wisely. Good values shape good lives.

What We Teach

Volunteering, civic sense and etiquette, with the opportunity for service awards that recognize community contributions.

The Age-by-Age Progression

Character grows from small, consistent choices repeated over years, not weeks.

Year 1

Foundations

Ages 10–12
  • Practicing everyday kindness, manners, and basic civic sense
  • Volunteering alongside family or through school programs
  • Learning what "Seva" (selfless service) means in simple terms
  • Beginning to notice how the company they keep affects their choices
Year 2

Building

Ages 13–15
  • Joining or helping organize a structured volunteering project
  • Practicing accountability and follow-through in group commitments
  • Understanding "Satsang" (choosing friendships and company thoughtfully)
  • Building consistent civic habits: etiquette, respect, community awareness
Year 3

Mastery

Ages 16–18
  • Designing and leading a community service project from start to finish
  • Earning recognition through sustained volunteering and service awards
  • Mentoring younger kids in character, service, and civic responsibility
  • Reflecting on identity and values, and how to give back for life, not just for a program

Topics in This Track

  • Giving Back through Meaningful Volunteering (Seva)
  • Civic Sense & Etiquette
  • Service Awards
