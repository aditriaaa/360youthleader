Money Smarts

Earn, save, invest: practical finance for young people.

Why This Track Matters

Most of us learned about money the hard way. The Gita's karma yoga reminds us: do the work, give your best, don't be ruled by results. We teach our children to understand money early (how to earn it, save it, grow it, and respect it) so they have the freedom and confidence to make sound choices throughout their lives.

What We Teach

Personal finance and investing basics, entrepreneurship, side hustles, and home economics.

The Age-by-Age Progression

Financial confidence compounds: the earlier it starts, the further it goes.

Year 1

Foundations

Ages 10–12
  • Understanding what money is, where it comes from, and how it's earned
  • Learning the difference between needs and wants
  • Starting basic saving habits with an allowance or small earnings
  • Practicing simple budgeting for a small goal
Year 2

Building

Ages 13–15
  • Managing a real budget from allowance, gifts, or a first job
  • Learning the basics of saving and an introduction to how investing works
  • Practicing home economics: cooking, simple household budgeting
  • Exploring the idea of a side hustle or small entrepreneurial project
Year 3

Mastery

Ages 16–18
  • Opening and managing a bank account independently
  • Understanding credit, interest, and the basics of investing
  • Exploring entrepreneurship or running a real side hustle
  • Preparing financially for life after high school (college costs, independence, first jobs)
