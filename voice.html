Voice, Brand & Influence

Speak clearly, tell stories, build a personal brand and influence well.

Why This Track Matters

Many of us grew up shy about speaking up — and we know how much that held us back. We help children express themselves clearly, with confidence and humility, so they're never afraid to share what they think or who they are.

What We Teach

Public speaking, storytelling, persuasive writing, publishing, personal brand, and mentorship.

The Age-by-Age Progression

A confident voice is trained, not born. Here's how we build it, year by year.

Year 1

Foundations

Ages 10–12
  • Speaking up confidently in small group settings and class discussions
  • Learning the basic structure of a story: beginning, middle, end
  • Practicing respectful disagreement and expressing an opinion clearly
  • Building comfort with being seen and heard
Year 2

Building

Ages 13–15
  • Delivering structured public speeches in front of real audiences
  • Learning persuasive writing techniques for essays and presentations
  • Discovering and practicing their own unique voice and style
  • Getting comfortable receiving and using constructive feedback
Year 3

Mastery

Ages 16–18
  • Building a genuine personal brand across writing, speech, and presence
  • Publishing original work — a blog, an article, a book chapter
  • Speaking persuasively in high-stakes settings: interviews, competitions, leadership roles
  • Mentoring younger students in communication and public speaking
